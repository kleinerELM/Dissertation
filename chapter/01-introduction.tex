\section{Background and Motivation}

Portland cement is the most widely used binder to produce concrete.
The reactivity is determined by its composition and particle size.
To start a reaction called hydration.
During hydration, the cement starts to dissolve and precipitate new crystals which form a new micro-structure.
This micro-structure defines the properties of the resulting concrete.

This dissertation is devoted to improve the characterization the micro-structure of unhydrated and hydrated cement based binders utilizing a set of imaging techniques like scanning electron microscopy and computer tomography.
The goal is to better understand the influence of the compositon on the stucture of the hydration products. Therefore, the existing analytical methods had to be improved and further developed to be reproducible and automatable.

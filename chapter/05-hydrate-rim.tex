\chapter{hydrate rim measurements}


\section{Results}

The in section \ref{subsec:hrm} presented method was applied to embedded and polished alite and belite specimens of different ages.
The datasets differ in the imaged area and slightly in SEM imaging parameters.
For example, the accelerating voltage used varied from 10 to 15\,kV.
An overview of the images used is shown in Tables XX and YY.% \ref{tab:c3s}.
The raw images are publicly available %\cite{Kleiner.2023b}.

Belite shows a significant slower hydration, which is also visible in the hydrate rim growth (Figure \ref{fig:hydration-rim-results}).
After 28 days, Belite still shows smaller hydration rims than alite after 7 days.
